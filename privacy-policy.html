📜 Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 15, 2026

1. Introduction

AR Drawing Sketchflow ("the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and handle your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have control over your data.

2. Information We Collect

To provide secure authentication and account management features, we collect the following data:

  • Personal Information: We collect your Email Address when you create an account. This is used solely for account management, authentication, and communication regarding your account.
  • Device & Other IDs: We collect Device IDs and specific identifiers to maintain secure user sessions and prevent fraud.
  • User IDs: A unique User ID is assigned to your account to associate your saved preferences and app data with your login.

3. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services that may collect and process data on our behalf:

  • Firebase Authentication (Google): Used to manage user logins and secure account data. Firebase may collect IP addresses, User Agents, and Device IDs to prevent abuse. Learn more about Firebase Privacy.
  • Google AdMob: Used to display advertisements. AdMob may collect approximate location, ad interaction data, and device identifiers to show relevant ads.

4. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected data for the following purposes:

  • App Functionality: To log you in, save your preferences, and maintain your account state.
  • Security & Fraud Prevention: To detect suspicious login attempts and protect user accounts.
  • Account Management: To allow you to reset passwords or recover your account.

5. Permissions Used

The App requires the following permissions:

  • Camera: To capture photos/videos for the AR drawing overlay.
  • Storage: To save your artwork and import images for tracing.
  • Internet: Required for Firebase Authentication and loading advertisements.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your account data (email and User ID) only as long as your account is active.

Request Deletion: You have the right to delete your account and all associated data at any time. You can do this by:

  • Visiting our Account Deletion Request Page
  • Contacting us directly at our support email.

Upon request, your account and personal data will be permanently deleted from our servers within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. If we discover such data has been collected, we will delete it immediately.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us:
